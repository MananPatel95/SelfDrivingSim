# PR #20 — Right-hand lanes and intersection stop/yield rules for all cars

Merged 2026-10-12. GitHub: `MananPatel95/SelfDrivingSim#20`.

## What changed

North-American **right-hand traffic** plus a seeded mix of intersection controls, obeyed by the ego **and every AI traffic car**.

- Routes, spawn, and lane geometry sit in the right-hand lane (`LANE_OFFSET = 3.5` m from the yellow centerline). `rightLaneRoute` cuts the near corner on a right turn and goes wide into the far right lane on a left.
- Intersections are a deterministic ~1/3 mix of all-way **STOP**, **YIELD** (minor road only), and **uncontrolled** (`pickControl(i, j, seed)`). Seed **3** (the browser default) puts an all-way stop at the origin.
- 3D stop and yield signs stand on the right of each signed approach; painted stop lines mark signed approaches. Tesla vision labels them `stop_sign` / `yield_sign`.
- Shared `decideIntersection` + `StopBook`: full stop then first-come/first-served at stops (arrival-time ties yield to the right); yield to major-road traffic at yields; yield-to-right and to pedestrians when uncontrolled.
- HUD **Detections** on Tesla counts `detectVisibleAgents` boxes (vision, including signs), not leftover lidar tracks (`hudDetectionCount`).
- Parked in manual: AI View banner is **Manual**, not “Cruising: lane clear”.
- Minimap marks stop (red square), yield (yellow triangle), uncontrolled (grey dot).
- Query params `?seed=` (default 3) and `?demo=lane|stop|yield|mix|queue` for reproducible shots.

`generateCityMap` no longer fills `trafficLights`. `updateTrafficLights` and the planner’s red-light check still exist but run on an empty list, so they are not the live intersection control.

## Why

Cars were straddling the yellow line and treating every junction as a 30 s NS/EW traffic-light cycle. The city needed right-hand lanes and a visible, shared stop/yield/uncontrolled mix.

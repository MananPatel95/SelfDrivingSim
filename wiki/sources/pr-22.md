# PR #22 — Kenney sedan GLB and Tesla-style cockpit

## What changed

Replaced the procedural cigar-pod ego mesh with Kenney Car Kit `sedan.glb` (CC0) loaded via `GLTFLoader`. Silver paint, ~4.7 × 1.85 × 1.45 m. Named wheel nodes sit in the arches, touch the road, steer and spin. First person is a left-of-frame seat at ~1.2 m: thin dash, centre screen, 36 cm wheel (~450° A/D), thin A-pillars, no roof tint.

## Why

Visual review rejected the procedural hull (balloon wheels, floating glass, slab dash).

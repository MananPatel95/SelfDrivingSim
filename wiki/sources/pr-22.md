# PR #22 — Kenney sedan-sports GLB and Tesla-style cockpit

## What changed

Replaced the procedural cigar-pod ego mesh with Kenney Car Kit `sedan-sports.glb` (CC0) loaded via `GLTFLoader`. Fitted to ~4.7 × 1.85 × 1.45 m. Body-paint UV cells are tinted silver; only greenhouse window faces are dark glass. Lights, trim and tyres keep the original colormap. Named wheel nodes sit in the arches, touch the road, steer and spin. First person is a left-of-frame seat at ~1.2 m: full-width dash (bottom ~28%), hood/cowl sliver, A-pillars into a roof header, landscape ~15" centre tablet, steering column, 36 cm wheel with a rim 12 o'clock mark (~450° A/D). Extra stills: `?shot=cabin-wide`, `?shot=exterior-hood`.

## Why

Visual review rejected the procedural hull (balloon wheels, floating glass, slab dash) and then the boxy Kenney `sedan` plus a floating thin-dash cockpit.

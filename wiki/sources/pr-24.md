# PR #24 — Fix AI traffic deadlock: random walk, deadlock breaker, never reverse

Opened 2026-10-12. GitHub: `MananPatel95/SelfDrivingSim#24`. Branch `cursor/traffic-flow-6c1c`.

## What changed

AI traffic **random-walks the road graph** instead of riding one full-map lane until it died. At each intersection a car picks a weighted left / straight / right (no U-turn unless a dead end) and turns into the right-hand lane.

`decideIntersection` now: never stops a car already in the box; holds a go decision ~0.8 s; if every waiter has waited > ~3 s, the earliest arrival goes. NPC IDM is same-heading only and includes the ego. Reverse-direction lanes span the whole map. Pedestrian yield is roadway/crossing only (the 12 m box from [[pr-20]] is superseded). Stuck cars replan or respawn off-screen.

`updateVehicle`, `removeGround`, and `createBEVOccupancyFromAgents` were not replaced. `simHotPath.ts` is unused.

## Why

After [[pr-20]], cars froze in intersections (cyclic yield-to-right, cross-traffic IDM, reverse lanes ending at the origin, `laneProgress >= 1` freeze). Ego autopilot sat at 0 km/h behind them.

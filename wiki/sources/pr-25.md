# PR #25 — Tesla first-person cockpit, HUD panels, autopilot re-engage

## What changed

First-person (hood) camera is **locked** to the left-seat eye anchors on the body pivot (~1.2 m, **~88° FOV**, **~11° look-down** so the horizon sits ~40% from the top). The Kenney sedan GLB stays on `LAYER_EXTERIOR` and is not drawn; debug / lidar viz is hidden in this mode. A Tesla-style cockpit overlay (`LAYER_COCKPIT`) draws **only** a **thin horizontal dash shelf** with an ambient strip (dash top **~72%** down the frame so the road fills the view), a landscape tablet **in the dash to the right of the wheel**, and a 36 cm round wheel **0.6 m** ahead of the eye (flat oval hub, two thin spokes + lower V, small TESLA plate, ~450° A/D lock). **No roof header and no A-pillars** — nothing rises above the dash line except the top of the wheel rim. The overlay is ~21 draw calls (budget 36); low FPS in cloud screenshots is software GL on the VM, not extra geometry.

Right-side HUD cards (stack explainer, AI View, info) are a single column: collapse, close, drag by header, positions in `localStorage`. **P** resets a customised layout or toggles the column. Layout tests assert no overlapping rects at 1280×720 and 1920×1080, and no cover of the minimap, speed HUD, or centre banner.

**T** and the centre **Manual** banner re-engage autopilot: `reengageAutopilot()` rebuilds a right-lane route from the current pose to the trip destination (or cruises ahead with no trip), then the planner and stop/yield rules run as usual. Held WASD after T does not immediately steal control.

## Why

Live first-person (not the staged `?shot=cabin` stills) sat outside the cabin, showed the Kenney roof, a floating half-ring wheel, and overlapping HUD cards. WASD takeover had no path back to Auto.

# PR #27 — Dry ABS sedan braking (~1 g)

## What changed

Tuned `updateVehicle` in place (not replaced). Sedan `brakeForce` raised so full pedal saturates an ABS clamp at peak μ (~1.0 g). Brake pressure rises in ~0.12 s. Engine overrun on throttle lift. Standstill hold with no creep. GTA reverse waits ~0.28 s of S held after stopping. Handbrake still skips ABS so Space can skid. Planner comfort 3 m/s² / emergency 9 m/s² map through `vehicleInputFromAccel`. Stopping Dist HUD uses the ABS peak plus the pressure-rise delay.

Measured dry panic stops: 50 km/h **10.2 m** (was 14.6 m); 100 km/h **39.2 m** (was 56.2 m).

## Why

Full pedal felt weak and slidey compared with a real car (36–40 m from 100 km/h with ABS).

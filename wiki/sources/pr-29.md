# PR #29 — Impulse collisions, snap, and pedestrian tumble

## What changed

- Ego vs cars, poles, signs, pedestrians, buildings, and trees uses mesh-matched OBBs / upright capsules and a spatial-hash broadphase.
- Car-to-car: impulse with mass, restitution, momentum conservation, and yaw from off-centre contacts. Traffic cars get shoved (`impactCooldown`).
- Poles and signs stay solid when slow; above `SNAP_SPEED` (6 m/s) they fall over and the ego is slowed hard.
- Pedestrians tumble and the hit is logged. HUD **Collisions** increments once per object. Autopilot drops to manual (`takeover('collision')`).
- `updateVehicle`, `removeGround`, and `createBEVOccupancyFromAgents` were not replaced. Lidar `cast_rays` stays in Rust/WASM. `simHotPath.ts` is still unused.
- After [[pr-28]] merged to main, this branch kept the roadside-static `obstacleAhead` skip (poles / trees / signs / buildings do not full-brake autopilot) while contact still uses the impulse solver.

# PR #30 — Pink roadside destination + pull-over

## What changed

Map trips end at a **hot-pink roadside pad** (`#ff2d8a`), not in the driving-lane center.

- `tripRoute` = `rightLaneRoute` + a lateral ease and a road-parallel tail onto `PULL_OVER_OFFSET` (then 5.4 m; **superseded** by 5.775 m / ~0.3 m curb gap in [[pr-33]]). Parks heading-aligned, body on asphalt. Junction clicks slide the pad backward.
- Opposite-side / wrong-curb placement and the in-lane 0 km/h cruise-stop are **superseded** by [[pr-33]] (legal U-turn + `snapTripEnd`).
- On the pad: `arrived` holds the brakes, banner **Arrived**, Control **ARRIVED**. The 3D pin sits at the front-right of the pad (then 8.4×2.4 m; **superseded** by 8.4×2.45 m flush-to-curb in [[pr-33]]) and hides when parked.
- Tests: parked heading error < 5°, every sedan corner on asphalt (`|x| < 7` — **superseded** by heading-aware `onArrivalAsphalt` in [[pr-33]]), live sim holds Arrived.
- Minimap: the same `#ff2d8a` pin whenever a trip exists, including manual.
- `?demo=arrive` is a short mid-block northbound trip for shots.

## Why

The purple route already showed where the stack was going, but there was no destination object on the road, and arrival stopped in the travel lane.

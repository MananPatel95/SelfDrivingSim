# PR #37 — Restore perception overlay animation (GPU sweep, 10 Hz lerp)

## What changed

After [[pr-34]] the F1/F2/F3 overlays were correct but static: perception and `InstancedMesh` buffers only ticked at 10 Hz, and the lidar beam stayed fixed (no shimmer). This PR keeps that scheduling and the fixed beam, then animates the *display*. GitHub already used #35 (city look) and #36 (spawn / menu) on `main`; this is the overlay-animation follow-up.

- 10 Hz snapshots of tracks, vision boxes, occupancy grids, and lidar points. Each render frame interpolates poses / occupancy (`snapshotLerp.ts`) and drives motion with shader uniforms (`uTime`, `uSweep`, `uAlpha`). Perception is not recomputed between ticks.
- **F1 Tesla Vision:** eight camera frustums, occupancy-network voxels, vision boxes. **No lidar cloud.** Based on Tesla Vision / Occupancy Network (AI Day 2022, CVPR 2022 workshop, Tesla’s 2022 USS-removal note).
- **F2 Waymo Driver:** rotating lidar sweep; **world-anchored** points appear at the beam and decay ~0.3 s (they stay on the road); expanding radar pings; fused boxes with velocity arrows/trails; HD-map lane dashes. Based on the 6th-gen Driver blog (13 cameras, 4 lidars, 6 radars, Aug 2024) and the mapping handbook (2020).
- **F3 Waabi occupancy:** lidar sweep as the occupancy **input**; heatmap lerps; +1s/+2s/+3s ghost voxels; trajectory fan. Based on UnO / DIO occupancy forecasting and DeTra trajectories. Live occupancy is still the agent splat, not a learned field.
- `cast_rays` stays in Rust/WASM. `updateVehicle`, `removeGround`, `createBEVOccupancyFromAgents`, the planner, city textures, start menu, and `simHotPath.ts` were not rewritten.
- Tests: `snapshotLerp.test.ts` plus a visualizer case that `animate()` does not walk occupancy cells.

## Why

Users could not see what each stack perceives in real time. The old “life” was mostly per-frame lidar jitter (removed on purpose in #34). Sweep + decay + snapshot lerp restores motion without bringing the shimmer back.

## Perf (headless Chrome, SwiftShader, 1280×720, Look Low, `?demo=mix&seed=3`)

| Stack | FPS | HUD FPS | overlay ms | viz ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 Tesla | 7.62 | 8 | 0.5 | 0.0 |
| F2 Waymo | 7.38 | 7 | 0.5 | 0.3 |
| F3 Waabi | 7.21 | 8 | 0.6 | 0.3 |

F1/F2/F3 stay within ~5% of each other. CPU overlay/viz work is ≤0.6 ms (same band as [[pr-34]] overlay 0.3–1.2 ms). Absolute SwiftShader FPS is capture-noise-dominated versus the #34 Look Low ~10.3 number (different freeze/orbit/load). Hardware GL should sit with F1.

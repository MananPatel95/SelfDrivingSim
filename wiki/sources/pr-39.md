# PR #39 — Left turn cheaper than U-turn plus a right

## What changed

- `resolveTripDestination` sets `opposite` only when start and dest share a grid road **and** the click is left of that road's naive approach (`OPPOSITE_SIDE_LAT` −1.2 m). A cross-street dest that only needs a left / right / straight does not flip heading or insert `intersectionUTurn`.
- Cross-street pads call `slideOffIntersection(..., keepClickSide)` so a dest just past the corner stays on that arm. Same-street junction clicks still slide *backward* (park before the box).
- A legal left at an intersection stays near-Manhattan length — cheaper than going past, U-turning, and coming back for a right.
- Same-road opposite-side U-turn from [[pr-33]] is unchanged (`(0,22) → (−4.5,78)` still goes past the next junction).
- Screenshot rigs: `?demo=left-near`, `?ab=sx,sy,ex,ey`.

## Why

The A→B map sometimes planned a dest one left away by going through the corner, U-turning, and returning for a right. #33 treated “left of the last Manhattan segment” as opposite even when that segment was a different street; sliding the pad backward then put the pin on the wrong arm.
